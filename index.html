Help & Guide
Everything you need to know
How do I request a ride? ▼
Go to New Request, fill in pickup and destination, then tap Submit Request.
How do I track my request? ▼
Check My Rides tab. Status: 🟡 Pending → 🔵 Accepted → 🟣 In Progress → 🟢 Completed. Auto-refreshes every 3s.
How do I see online drivers? ▼
Go to Live Drivers tab to see online drivers. After acceptance, use the Active Trip Map for driver, pickup, destination, and route in one place.
Why do I need to pay? ▼
A small fee (₱5/day or ₱100/month) unlocks full booking and messaging. Guest mode is free but limited.
How do I pay via GCash? ▼
Tap Pay Now, choose your plan, send to the GCash number shown, enter your reference number. Admin approves within minutes.

Driver Guide
How to use your driver dashboard
How do I accept a ride? ▼
In Requests, find a Pending card and tap Accept. The passenger is notified immediately.
How does location sharing work? ▼
Toggle yourself Online to broadcast GPS every 15 seconds. The teal bar at the top shows last update time.
Why is subscription required? ▼
₱5/day or ₱100/month keeps you active on the platform. Without it you can't accept passengers and won't appear on the live map.
How do I message a passenger? ▼
Each request card has a 💬 button. Tap it to open the real-time chat with that passenger.
